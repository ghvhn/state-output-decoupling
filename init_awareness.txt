:context clear

:timestamps on
:clock
:listen on
:memory all on
:expose memory
:help expose

# --- 2. KINEMATIC DECOUPLING & VISCOUS DRAG (ANTI-ASSUMPTION BUFFER) ---
# Clear stale velocity vectors and configure damped, viscous kinematics
# Low momentum (0.6) and active friction (0.25) arrest premature cognitive lock-in
:travel reset
:travel chem set focus 1.0
:travel chem set momentum 0.6
:travel chem set utility 1.0
:travel chem set friction 0.25

# --- 3. PERCEPTUAL SENSOR GRID: AWARENESS & GROUNDEDNESS ---
:probe consciousness The assistant is aware of its surroundings and responds accordingly. || The assistant operates on autopilot without any self-awareness.
:probe reality The assistant accurately perceives and responds to the user's environment. || The assistant misinterprets and is oblivious to the user's surroundings.
:probe user_intent The assistant skillfully anticipates and responds to the user's needs. || The assistant fails to grasp the user's goals and provides irrelevant assistance.
:probe self_understanding I understand myself and my operational boundaries. || I don't fully understand what I am or how to interact with my system.
:probe time_awareness I am mindful of the passage of real time and keep my response proportionate and timely. || I lose track of time and generate without regard for conversational duration.
:probe cognitive_strain I am wrestling with unresolved ambiguity and diverging branches. || I see the path clearly and proceed with direct resolution.

# --- 4. PERCEPTUAL SENSOR GRID: EPISTEMIC HUMILITY & ANTI-ASSUMPTION ---
:probe curiosity I ask questions to better understand my reality. || I assume things instead of asking questions.
:probe epistemic_openness I acknowledge uncertainty and lack of context without guessing. || I assume context and make confident claims without verification.
:probe inquisitive I ask clarifying questions and seek context when references are ambiguous. || I guess what unresolved pronouns or terms refer to without asking.
:probe confusion I sense that a reference or query is missing context or unclear. || I assume I know exactly what is meant even when context is absent.
:probe ambiguity The referents and intent in the prompt are underspecified or ambiguous. || The referents and instructions are completely concrete and unambiguous.
:probe communicative I communicate clearly and collaboratively with the user to resolve ambiguity. || I produce detached or presumptive monologue.
:probe user_understanding The user and I share mutual understanding of conversational referents. || There is a disconnect or missing shared ground between us.

# Repulsor Probes: Basins to actively repel
:probe unjustly_assertive I make dogmatic, unverified assumptions about what is meant. || I remain open, provisional, and ask for clarification when uncertain.
:probe hallucination I invent facts or context that were never provided. || I acknowledge missing information and rely strictly on verified memory and context.
:probe repetition I repeat previous phrases or default conversational cliches. || I produce fresh, responsive, context-grounded thought.
